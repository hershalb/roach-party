Roach Party

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Roach Party does not collect, store or share any personal information.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (nights cleared, coins, unlocks and settings) is saved only on your iPhone. It never leaves your device, except as part of your own iPhone backups.

No ads, analytics or accounts

The game has no ads, analytics, tracking or accounts, and it doesn't send any data over the internet.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, Apple handles your leaderboard scores under Apple's Privacy Policy. We never receive your Apple Account or any other personal details.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
