FAQ

How do I turn off sound or vibration?

Tap the gear on the home screen and switch off Sound or Haptics.

How do I start over?

Tap the gear on the home screen, then Reset Progress. This erases your nights, coins and unlocks on this device and can't be undone.

Will my progress move to a new iPhone?

Your progress is saved on your iPhone and comes along when you restore a backup onto a new one.

Why can't I see the leaderboard?

Sign in to Game Center in the Settings app, then open Trophies again.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. Every coin is earned by splatting bugs.

A night feels impossible. Any tips?

Replay earlier nights to earn coins, then visit the Shop. A bigger weapon covers more bugs per tap, Thick Skin makes the Gross-Out meter drain faster, and Panic Button gives you an Exterminator charge every night for emergencies.
