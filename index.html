Roach Party

Tap. Splat. Survive the night.

Coming soon to the App Store

Your apartment has a roach problem. Tonight, they're throwing a party. Armed with nothing but a flip-flop and a pair of heart boxers, defend your home across 50 increasingly ridiculous nights. Tap to splat, don't let them touch you, and whatever you do, don't let the Gross-Out meter fill up.

25 gross bugs

Speedy roaches, ant trails, hopping spiders, exploding bombardier beetles, and ladybugs you should NOT squash.

5 ridiculous bosses

The Roach King, the Lord of the Flies, the Spider Queen, the Termite Queen and the Roach Emperor himself.

Special nights

Party Night, Lights Out, Midnight Snack and Golden Hour shake things up just when you've got it handled.

Gear up

Earn coins for the Slipper, Rolled Newspaper, Croc, Boot, Frying Pan and the legendary Golden Sandal.
